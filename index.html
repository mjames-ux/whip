Terms & Privacy

1. Independent Civic Service

Whip is an independent civic information website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the UK Government, the House of Commons, or UK Parliament.

2. Automated Summaries & Accuracy

Daily and weekly briefings are generated automatically from official Hansard debates, Written Questions, and Commons Votes data. While designed to remain strictly neutral and factual, automated summaries may occasionally omit procedural nuance. Always consult the linked verbatim parliament.uk transcript before relying on information for journalistic, legal, or campaign purposes.

3. Parliamentary Data Licence

Contains Parliamentary information licensed under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0. Postcode lookups use open boundary data via Postcodes.io.

4. Privacy & Local Storage

Whip uses no advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or user accounts. Your 10-MP watchlist is stored exclusively on your own device in browser localStorage and is never transmitted to Whip’s servers.
