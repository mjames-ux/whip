Liberal Democrat / Epsom and Ewell

Helen Maguire

MP for Epsom and Ewell • 1 Active Day

Mon 16 Nov: Helen Maguire did not speak in the main House of Commons Chamber on Monday, 16 November 2026. However, she carried out parliamentary duties as a member of the Joint Committee on the National Security Strategy, taking part in scrutiny regarding the UK's satellite services.

Context

The session examined the potential threats to the UK's defence, security, and economy should satellite services be lost, noting that an estimated 18% of the UK's Gross Domestic Product is underpinned by satellites. This committee work forms part of ongoing parliamentary oversight into national infrastructure resilience and modern security vulnerabilities.
