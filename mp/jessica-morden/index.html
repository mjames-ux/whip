Labour / Newport East

Jessica Morden

MP for Newport East · 2 Votes

Jessica Morden did not speak in the main Commons Chamber during the week of 23 Nov 2026, focusing on other parliamentary duties.

In the voting lobbies, Jessica Morden took part in 2 divisions, voting Aye on Finance Bill and voting No on Finance Bill: Reasoned Amendment to Second Reading.

Why this matters

The Finance Bill sets out the government's plans for taxation and public spending for the year ahead. Voting on its second reading determines whether the core principles of these national financial measures can proceed to detailed legislative scrutiny.
