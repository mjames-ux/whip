Valerie Vaz

MP for Walsall and Bloxwich · —

Valerie Vaz did not speak in the main Commons Chamber during the week of 23 Nov 2026, focusing on other parliamentary duties.

In the voting lobbies, Valerie Vaz took part in 2 divisions, voting Aye on Finance Bill and voting No on Finance Bill: Reasoned Amendment to Second Reading.

Why this matters

The Finance Bill turns taxation plans and budget proposals into binding UK law. Passing its second reading allows the bill to move forward to detailed examination in committee. This process helps ensure Parliament can formally scrutinize national taxation and public spending plans.
