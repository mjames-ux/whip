Legal & Data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What Whip. Does

Whip. provides plain-English daily and weekly summaries of House of Commons activity compiled from official public parliamentary records (including Hansard, Commons Votes, Written Questions, and GOV.UK). You can browse MP briefings and profiles without creating an account.

2. Information We Collect & Store

  • Sunday Briefing Subscriptions: If you sign up for weekly email updates, we store your email address and the MP(s) or constituency you selected so we can send your briefing and process one-click unsubscribes. We never sell or rent subscriber emails.
  • Postcode Lookups: When you enter a UK postcode to find your MP, your postcode is sent to the public postcodes.io service to resolve the parliamentary constituency. We do not attach postcodes to personal profiles.
  • Watchlist & Browser Storage: Saved MPs on your Watchlist are stored locally in your browser via localStorage rather than on a user account server.

Cookies & Local Storage

Manage Storage & Non-Essential Preferences

Whip. does not use third-party advertising cookies. Essential browser storage is used only when needed for core site navigation; optional watchlist persistence in localStorage can be opted out below at any time.

3. Your Rights & Contact

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your subscription record, or unsubscribe immediately using the link included in every Sunday email.
